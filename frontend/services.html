Our work

🏠

Elderly Care and Support

Home-based caregiving, daily living support, meal provision, respite care and safer living arrangements for older adults.

💙

Medical Outreach Programs

Free screenings, medication support, referrals and health education to address chronic illness in underserved communities.

🤝

Counselling and Emotional Support

Companionship, peer support and one-on-one counselling that reduce loneliness and strengthen mental wellbeing.

⚖

Food and Welfare Distribution

Nutritious food, household essentials and emergency welfare support for vulnerable older persons and families in need.

🌍

Community Development Initiatives

Awareness campaigns, local support networks and intergenerational programmes that revive communal responsibility.

�

Advocacy and Awareness

We promote elder rights, improved care systems and stronger social protection for older adults and vulnerable communities.

Our commitment

Personalized care that respects dignity, health and belonging.

SEWI understands that no two older adults have the same story, needs or goals. Our support is designed to respond with empathy, practical assistance and steady human presence while building stronger, more inclusive communities.

  • Support shaped around the individual and their family context.
  • Respectful engagement with compassion and cultural sensitivity.
  • Programs rooted in health, nutrition, dignity and social inclusion.
  • Partnership-driven action that strengthens long-term care pathways.
