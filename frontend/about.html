About us

Our story

Founded in 2017, Sewielders has proudly been a trusted name in senior and elder care.

We believe that older adults deserve not just care, but dignity, comfort, inclusion and meaningful connection.

Our work began with a clear need: to create a compassionate support system that helps seniors and elders thrive in a safe and empowering environment.

Explore our work
Our mission

We provide outstanding and creative services to individuals within our care, tailoring our approach to address each person’s distinct needs.

  • Support older adults with consistent, compassionate daily care.
  • Promote safety, dignity and social wellbeing.
  • Respond to the emotional and practical needs of families and communities.
Our values

Care grounded in respect, compassion and community.

  • Dignity and respect in every interaction.
  • Holistic wellbeing that includes emotional and social support.
  • Service informed by empathy, trust and inclusion.
