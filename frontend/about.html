About us

Our story

Support to the Elderly and Weak Initiative (SEWI) is committed to improving the quality of life for elderly individuals and vulnerable populations.

Founded to address the realities facing older Nigerians, SEWI works to restore dignity, promote inclusion and enhance wellbeing for seniors and the weak.

In Nigeria, many older adults are faced with poverty, limited access to affordable healthcare, chronic conditions, social isolation and inadequate formal support systems. SEWI responds with compassionate, community-led programmes that provide care, advocacy and practical support where it is most needed.

Explore our work
Our mission

To provide sustainable support services, healthcare access and empowerment programmes for the elderly and vulnerable communities through partnerships, innovation and community engagement.

  • Support the elderly and weak with practical, compassionate interventions.
  • Improve access to health care, food support and community care.
  • Build inclusive programmes that respond to age, disability and social vulnerability.
Our vision

To build a compassionate society where every elderly and vulnerable individual lives with dignity, security and access to essential care.

  • Protect human dignity and respect for older adults.
  • Strengthen care systems through collaboration and accountability.
  • Create a future where no elder is forgotten or left unsupported.
